## Two outcomes we aim at

Everything in Compass serves two measurable outcomes. Each one is traced from an insight to a specific customer need and to a number you can re-measure.

| | Outcome 1: no surprise shortfalls | Outcome 2: more money set aside on purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Customer need | A gig worker or student who cannot tell on the 10th whether the bike payment on the 24th will bounce | Someone who "saves what is left", which is usually nothing |
| Compass insight | The 30-day forecast finds the first day the balance falls below the safety buffer and says how far below | The savings part of the health score counts only saving done on purpose, and says how much more would reach the 20% target |
| What the person is prompted to do | "Your balance may dip to Rs -120 around 24 Oct": move a bill, add less, or set money aside now (forecast nudge, tip of the day) | Turn on round-ups, or put a small fixed amount into a goal (tip of the day, goal nudge) |
| Number we measure | Days of warning before the first low-balance day; shortfall days avoided in a pilot | Rs set aside per month and savings rate (savings change / income) |
| Measured here (simulated) | See "Headline outcomes" below | See "Headline outcomes" below |
| Needs real data | Whether the person acted and the dip did not happen | Whether round-ups stay on and the savings rate rises over time |

The trace in the app: forecast or score computes the insight, a nudge or the tip of the day states it in plain words in English or Bangla, and one tap goes to the screen where the person acts (Forecast, Goals, Budgets). Whether people act on it can only be measured with real users in a pilot; nothing here claims it.

## Headline outcomes (measured from the simulated data)

| Persona | Outcome 1: warning before the first low-balance day (days) | Outcome 2: change set aside / month with round-ups (Rs) | ... as share of income |
|---|---|---|---|
| student | no dip in the next 30 days | 171 | 1.6% |
| gig | 1 | 164 | 0.5% |
| salaried | 28 | 89 | 0.2% |

Formulas: warning = days from today to the first forecast day below the safety buffer (0 = already below it today); round-up change = sum over outgoing payments of the gap to the next Rs 10, per month; share of income = that change / monthly income. Round-ups are an opt-in setting, so the second figure is what a person who turns them on would set aside, not what everyone does.
